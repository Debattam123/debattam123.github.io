Picard Group Matrix Calculator

Symbolic 2×2 matrix algebra over the Gaussian integers ℤ[i] — the setting of PSL(2, ℤ[i]).

Entries may be numbers (Gaussian integers such as 1+2i, 3-i, -2) or symbolic variables (a, b, c, …) and any expression built from them with + − * / ^ and parentheses. All algebra is exact and symbolic: products are expanded by the distributive law (a(b+c) = ab + ac), i² = −1, and division produces reduced rational expressions — so the inverse of {{a,b},{c,d}} comes out as d/(a·d − b·c), exactly like a Picard-group computation by hand.

Input format & notes
  • Numeric entries: Gaussian integers written as a+bi, e.g. 2, -3, i, -i, 4i, 1+2i, 3-i. Fractions like 1/2 are allowed too (kept exact).
  • Symbolic entries: any letters as variables, combined with + − * / ^ and parentheses, e.g. a, 2a+1, a*(b+c), (1+i)*z. Multiplication is expanded and like terms are collected automatically.
  • i is the imaginary unit with i² = −1; it is not a variable.
  • S, T, U are standard generators of PSL(2,ℤ[i]): S=[[0,-1],[1,0]], T=[[1,1],[0,1]], U=[[1,i],[0,1]].
  • Powers accept negative exponents (via the inverse) and 0 (identity).
  • Check Picard reports membership in PSL(2,ℤ[i]) for a numeric matrix (Gaussian-integer entries and determinant 1). For symbolic matrices it shows the symbolic determinant instead.
