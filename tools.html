Tools for Research

Small interactive programs written alongside my research. Each one is a single self-contained page: everything runs in the browser, no data is uploaded and no external libraries are loaded, so they also work offline once the page has been opened.

Axes of Loxodromic Elements in the Picard Group

Enter a matrix [[a,b],[c,d]] with ad − bc = 1 and, if the element is loxodromic, the tool draws its axis in hyperbolic 3-space — the geodesic joining its two fixed points on ∂H³ = ℂ ∪ {∞} — together with the closed geodesic the axis induces on the quotient orbifold H³/PSL(2, ℤ[i]), folded into the Bianchi fundamental domain.

It reports the trace, the eigenvalue of modulus greater than one, the translation length, the holonomy and the complex length, and tests reversibility through the invariant fA = (a−d)/c, searching the conjugacy class for representatives in the special normal form [[d+ck, b],[c, d]].

Hecke Triangle Group Axis Visualizer

Build a word g = a bε1 … a bεn in the Hecke triangle group Gp = ⟨S, Tλ⟩, λ = 2cos(π/p) — the (2, p, ∞) triangle group ≅ ℤ2 ∗ ℤp, generated by a = S of order 2 and b = STλ of order p. The tool classifies the product as hyperbolic, parabolic or elliptic and draws the axis as the semicircle on its two real fixed points — overlaid on the tessellation of ℍ by translates of the fundamental domain Fp = { |z| ≥ 1, |Re z| ≤ λ/2 }, whose width varies with λ.

A second panel folds one period of the axis back into Fp to show the closed geodesic on the Hecke orbifold, with the order-2 cone point i, the cone point of order p at ρp = eiπ/p and the cusp marked. Choosing p = 3 gives λ = 1 and recovers the modular group PSL(2, ℤ) exactly.

Picard Group Matrix Calculator

Exact symbolic 2×2 matrix algebra over the Gaussian integers ℤ[i]. Entries may be Gaussian integers such as 1+2i or symbolic variables, and any expression built from them with + − × / ^; products are expanded by the distributive law and division yields reduced rational functions, so nothing is ever approximated numerically.

Handles powers with negative exponents, the standard generators S, T, U of PSL(2, ℤ[i]), and a membership test reporting whether a numeric matrix lies in the Picard group.
